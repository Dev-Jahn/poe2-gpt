## Findings requiring independent implementation

- **Leech:** the current public [Life Leech description](https://poe2db.tw/us/Life_Leech) caps total hit damage at 40,000 while preserving damage-type ratios, applies monster leech resistance, recovers over one second, and allows one active Life Leech instance. PR #1938 instead caps each damage type's already-calculated leech amount at 40,000 and retains PoE1 rate-cap machinery. It is evidence of a missing feature, not a correct drop-in implementation.
- **Impale:** [Impale support](https://poe2db.tw/us/Impale) can inflict but cannot extract Impale. Its public version history describes damage granted to a subsequent attack hit. PR #1820 re-enables legacy stack/DPS machinery; extraction and source/consumer interactions need separate calculation.
- **Conditional modifiers:** PR #2378 exposes a real parser mismatch (`Curse` / `Mark` versus `Cursed` / `Marked`). PR #2510's on-kill trigger cannot establish continuous Onslaught merely because its chance is nonzero.
- **Time-Lost jewels:** [Baryanic Leylines](https://poe2db.tw/us/Baryanic_Leylines) increases the radius of non-unique Time-Lost jewels by 40%. The matching [Korean record](https://poe2db.tw/kr/Baryanic_Leylines) identifies it as 바리야 지맥, passive hash 36891. Unique jewels must remain unchanged; actual node inclusion needs a geometric boundary regression.
- **Mountain's Teachings:** the [public buff record](https://poe2db.tw/us/Mountains_Teachings) defines a damage threshold after mitigation but before other damage-taken modifiers. PR #2073 explicitly omits that mitigation and therefore is incomplete.
- **Rite of Passage:** [the item record](https://poe2db.tw/us/Rite_of_Passage) distinguishes player possession from enemy possession and records 0.5 player-skill changes. PR #2443's modifiers/default toggles do not establish the possessed skill rotation or uptime.

The current dev pin already contains the recently merged September 4 gameplay
changes. Among the screened recently updated closed PRs, #1894 was closed
without merge; its buff-duration proposal is not evidence of an accepted fix.
Existing imported character data remains authoritative for concrete item rolls.
